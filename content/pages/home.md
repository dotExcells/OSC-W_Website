## Bottom-Up Community

We are a **bottom-up community** from **Wageningen University & Research** (WUR) and associated research institutes.

Our multidisciplinary community of academics and students share a common goal: to stimulate **transparency**, **accessibility**, and **reproducibility** of **research**.

OSC-W is part of the International Network of Open Science & Scholarship Communities ([INOSC](https://osc-international.com/)).

## Who can join OSC-W?

All **researchers**, **students** and **support staff** of **WUR** and associated research institutes, including **NIOO-KNAW** and at all career stages, are welcome!

Becoming a member is free, simple and non-committal.
